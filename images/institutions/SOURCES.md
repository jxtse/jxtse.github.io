# Institution marks

These marks identify education and internship affiliations; they do not imply endorsement. All marks remain the property of their respective institutions.

- `nanjing-university.png`: [Nanjing University official website](https://www.nju.edu.cn/en/images/favicon.png), losslessly optimized.
- `peking-university.png`: crest cropped from the [Peking University official header](https://english.pku.edu.cn/Uploads/Bden/Picture/2020/11/25/s5fbdbf048bc00.png), resized to 128 px.
- `beijing-jiaotong-university.png`: rendered from the vector crest in [Michael7099/BJTU-LOGO](https://github.com/Michael7099/BJTU-LOGO/blob/master/BJTU-%E6%A0%A1%E5%BE%BD.pdf), checked against the [official university header](https://www.bjtu.edu.cn/images/img2019/logo_01.png), resized to 128 px.
- `mind-lab.png`: [Mind Lab official site icon](https://mindlab-web.oss-accelerate.aliyuncs.com/mindlab-favicon.10f1912ad725.png), linked by https://macaron.xin/mindlab, resized to 128 px.
- `microsoft.svg`: original symbol from [Microsoft's official branding assets](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/media/howto-add-branding-in-apps/ms-symbollockup_mssymbol_19.svg).
